- [designbriefs.ch](https://designbriefs.ch/) : Site collectant des “briefs” et exercices pour l’enseignement du design. Couvre diverses matières telles que: [Design d’interfaces](https://designbriefs.ch/keyword/interface-design/), [Photo](https://designbriefs.ch/keyword/photography/), [Vidéo](https://designbriefs.ch/keyword/video/), [Son](https://designbriefs.ch/keyword/sound/)...
